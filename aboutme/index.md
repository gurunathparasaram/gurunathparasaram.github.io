I'm a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with 6+ years of experience building large-scale, distributed systems for NLP, Generative AI and information retrieval.

## What I work on now

My current work focuses on LLM applications for healthcare and enterprise use cases: retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), information extraction, hybrid search, intent and entity recognition, prompt engineering, evaluation frameworks and scalable ML pipelines. I build AI-based tools for extracting, retrieving and analyzing information that cut processing time from hours to seconds and improve decision workflows.

I also present data insights and model performance findings to stakeholders, so that decisions are backed by data. Day to day, I work across large-scale data analysis, preprocessing, experimentation and production ML systems, mostly in Python, SQL and PySpark.

## NLP at Zoho

Before this, I was a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Zoho, working on NLP for large-scale writing-assistance products. My work there spanned the evolution of NLP itself: from classical ML, linguistic patterns and rule-based systems to neural sequence models and Transformers.

In Zoho's R&D Labs, I built contextual grammatical error correction for several products. It combined language-specific rules, pattern-based methods, data-driven models and Transformer models to improve precision while reducing false positives.

I worked across the full NLP lifecycle:

- Data collection and curation, including synthetic data generated with linguistic rules
- Model evaluation and error analysis
- Inference profiling and optimization
- API development and large-scale production deployment

These distributed NLP services handled hundreds of millions to billions of requests and user-written sentences. That work gave me strong foundations in MLOps, distributed inference optimization and system reliability.

## What I care about

I'm most interested in building practical AI systems that combine modern language models with classical NLP, deterministic rules, reliable evaluation, AI governance and strong production engineering.
